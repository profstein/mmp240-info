                <h2>Quick Guide: Importing Your Colours into Figma</h2>
                <ol>
                    <li>Make sure you've already generated your palette + font choices using the earlier AI prompts (you'll have a Realtime Colors link).</li>
                    <li>Open our class Figma template and go to the <strong>"Colors & Typography"</strong> page.</li>
                    <li>Install the <em>Realtime Colors</em> plugin in Figma. Click on the Assets icon. Type Realtime Colors in the searchbar. Install the plugin.</li>
                    <li>Run the plugin. Paste your link, and click <strong>Generate</strong>.</li>
                    <li>The plugin will automatically create a swatch frame and Color Styles inside the page.</li>
                </ol>
