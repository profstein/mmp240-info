## Prompt for Creating Your Project Brief

Use the following AI prompt to help you generate the background information for your Project Brief. This prompt will guide you step‑by‑step and then compile your answers into a clear, well‑structured summary document. Copy and paste the entire prompt into ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot.

---

**AI Prompt:**

I’m planning a website for my final project. Please guide me through these steps and then create a summary document based on my answers:

**Target Audience**
Ask me:
Who is your site for?
Why would they visit the site (What would they get out of it)?

**Mood/Vibe**
Ask me:
What feeling should visitors get when they land on your site?
Should the vibe be energetic, calm, bold, funky, or something else?

**Style Direction**
Use my Mood/Vibe answer to suggest styles that match (e.g., modern, playful, professional, minimal). Tell me what design approaches are common for that mood (simple, rich, minimal) and ask if that’s the direction I want.

**Color Direction**
Based on my Mood/Vibe and Style answers, suggest color palettes typically associated with similar sites. Explain why those colors fit the mood. Then ask me what colors I’d like to use.

**Typography Direction**
Use my previous answers to suggest typography styles that fit (modern, elegant, playful, professional). Ask if I prefer serif or sans-serif fonts, and let me know it’s okay if I’m unsure.

**Inspiration**
After I answer all questions, find 3–5 working links to websites that match my mood and style. Verify the links work. Provide a short note on what each site does well (layout, color, typography). Then ask me to pick the ones I like or share others I’ve found.

**Final Step**
Finally, write a well-structured document with headings for each section and my responses clearly organized. If I gave one-word or short responses, flesh them out into more complete sentences. Make the writing clear to someone who is reading it and didn't know the questions that were asked. Include the full URLs for all inspiration sites.

---

## Content Prompt (Improved – Use After the Background Prompt) 

Copy and paste the following into the **same AI chat window** after completing the background prompt. This version includes enough details about the project requirements so the AI can guide you properly.

---

**AI Content Prompt:

I’m planning a multi-page, responsive website for my final Web Design project. Please help me quickly generate a clear *starter content plan* based on the background info I already provided in this chat (audience, mood, style, colors, typography, and inspiration).

My project requirements:

* Topic must be a **Travel/Local Guide**, **Hobby Site**, or **How‑To Guide**
* Minimal original writing; curated/adapted content is fine
* 3–5 pages with consistent navigation
* At least one reused component (ex: cards, repeated sections)
* At least one simple interactive element (ex: accordion, lightbox)
* Images and embedded video encouraged
* Focus is on **design**, clarity, and responsiveness—not heavy content creation

Please guide me through these **short steps**, then create a compact content summary. **At every step, update your plan to match my chosen pages exactly. Do not keep any pages or sections I do not confirm. Always replace earlier suggestions with my final selections.**

### 1. Page List (Fast)

Ask me:

* Which 3–5 pages I want
* One sentence about what each page should do

Then: **Rewrite the page list using ONLY the pages I confirm. Delete all earlier suggestions.**

### 2. Sections (Fast)

For each page in **my confirmed list only**, suggest 2–4 sensible sections based on my topic. Ask me to confirm or adjust.
Examples: hero, intro, highlights, tips, gallery, steps, resources.

After I confirm: **Use only my approved sections and remove any unconfirmed ones.**

### 3. Light Content

For each approved section:

* Provide **very short placeholder text** I can refine later
* Suggest **1–2 possible images or videos** and where to source them (Pexels/YouTube)

### 4. Interaction

Suggest **one simple interactive element** that fits my topic. Ask me where it should go. Use only the location I approve.

### 5. Final Output

Create a short, clear summary with:

* My *final confirmed* page list
* Sections for each page (approved only)
* Short placeholder text
* Suggested media ideas
* Optional simple interaction

**Do not include any pages, sections, or ideas I did not confirm. Replace all earlier suggestions with my final choices.**

Make this concise and beginner‑friendly so I can move quickly into wireframing. Keep the writing brief so I can move quickly into wireframing.

## Prompt for Generating a Simple Site Map Diagram

After you complete your background prompt *and* your content prompt, you can optionally ask Copilot (GPT‑5) to generate a clean, black‑and‑white site map image for your project. Copilot can output normal **PNG images**, so this prompt asks it to draw a simple diagram that looks like a real site map.

Copy and paste the following prompt into **the same Copilot chat window** where you completed the earlier prompts:

---

**AI Site Map Prompt **

Using the final page list I confirmed earlier, create a **simple black‑and‑white, hierarchical, **site map diagram as a PNG image**. Follow these requirements:

* Put the **Home** page at the top.
* Beneath it, draw **one box for each page** in my final confirmed list.
* If any pages are sub‑pages/child pages, place them under their parent and connect them visually.
* Use clean rectangular boxes and connecting lines.
* Make sure the text labels inside each box are readable and match the pages we agreed on earlier.
* Keep the layout simple and beginner‑friendly.
* **Use ONLY the pages I confirmed**, and remove all earlier suggestions.

After generating the PNG diagram, include a brief one‑sentence explanation of the structure.

## Troubleshooting Tips for Copilot (GPT‑5)

If Copilot isn’t giving you the correct site map, try these fixes:

* **It added pages I didn’t ask for:**
  “Only use the pages I confirmed. Remove all others and regenerate the diagram.”

* **It ignored my sub‑pages:**
  “Show these as child pages under their parent and connect them visually.”

* **It changed my page names:**
  “Use my exact page names. Do not rename or paraphrase.”

* **The diagram is messy or hard to read:**
  “Please regenerate the PNG using a cleaner layout with evenly spaced boxes.”

* **It mixed old and new versions of my pages:**
  “Forget all previous versions and use ONLY my final confirmed page list from earlier in this chat.”

Use these prompts anytime Copilot drifts from your actual project structure. The goal is a simple, accurate PNG site map that matches *your* pages exactly.

### Quick Guide: Importing Your Colours into Figma

1. Make sure you’ve already generated your palette + font choices using the earlier AI prompts (you’ll have a Realtime Colors link).

2. Open our class Figma template and go to the **“Colors & Typography”** page.

3. Install the *Realtime Colors* plugin in Figma. Click on the Assets icon.  Type Realtime Colors in the searchbar. Install the plugin. 

4. Run the plugin. Paste your link, and click **Generate**.

5. The plugin will automatically create a swatch frame and Color Styles inside the page.
