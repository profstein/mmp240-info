Before You Begin: Create Your Creative Brief Document

You'll be building your Project 4 Creative Brief step-by-step as you work through each of the six [6] AI prompts. This document is a chance to collect all of your ideas in one place so you can later use it as a reference when you design your site.

1. Create Your Document

Use one of these templates:

  1. Google Docs Template: Make sure you're signed in to Google and then Click File > Make a Copy
  2. MS Word Template Click the download icon to download a copy.

The templates have the following sections

  • Project Title
  • Background
  • Content
  • Site Map
  • Color and Typography

Keep this document open the whole time.

2. After Each Prompt

After each of the five prompts — Background, Content, Site Map, Color Palette, and Typography — do the following:

  1. Review the AI's output
    Read through the response and keep asking for revisions until you like the result.
  2. Copy from the AI Chat. Paste it under the correct heading in your planning document.
    1. Edit freely after you paste: Add | Rewrite | Shorten | Lengthen | Clarify
    2. Delete anything that doesn't fit

If You Can’t Copy the Text

Sometimes Copilot or ChatGPT puts output in a format your computer won’t let you copy.

If this happens, paste the following line into the chat:

I can't copy your output. Please reformat the last response into a rich-text style format that I can copy, keeping simple formatting such as bold, bullets, and headings.

This usually forces the AI to output text that copies correctly.

This is your planning document — the AI is only a brainstorming partner. You can and should always change it to fit our writing style and vision.

Using AI to Help You Build Your Project Brief

You may use any AI tool you feel comfortable with to generate your Project Brief. Many of you already use ChatGPT, but remember that you also have access to Microsoft Copilot through your CUNY login.

To use Microsoft Copilot:

  • Go to https://copilot.microsoft.com
  • When asked which account to use, select your Work/School account, not a personal one
  • After logging in, you will automatically have access to GPT‑5
  • Copilot will also save your conversations, making it easier to revisit your work later

Once you've signed in, copy and paste the prompt below into the AI tool of your choice.

Prompt for Creating Your Project Brief [prompt 1]

Use the following AI prompt to help you generate the background information for your Project Brief. This prompt will guide you step‑by‑step and then compile your answers into a clear, well‑structured summary document.

I'm planning a website for my final project. Please guide me through these steps and then create a summary document based on my answers:

**Target Audience**
Ask me:
Who is your site for?
Why would they visit the site (What would they get out of it)?

**Mood/Vibe**
Ask me:
What feeling should visitors get when they land on your site?
Should the vibe be energetic, calm, bold, funky, or something else?

**Style Direction**
Use my Mood/Vibe answer to suggest styles that match (e.g., modern, playful, professional, minimal). Tell me what design approaches are common for that mood (simple, rich, minimal) and ask if that's the direction I want.

**Color Direction**
Based on my Mood/Vibe and Style answers, suggest color palettes typically associated with similar sites. Explain why those colors fit the mood. Then ask me what colors I'd like to use.

**Typography Direction**
Use my previous answers to suggest typography styles that fit (modern, elegant, playful, professional). Ask if I prefer serif or sans-serif fonts, and let me know it's okay if I'm unsure.

**Inspiration**
After I answer all questions, find 3–5 working links to websites that match my mood and style. Verify the links work. Provide a short note on what each site does well (layout, color, typography). Then ask me to pick the ones I like or share others I've found.

**Final Step**
Finally, write a well-structured document with headings for each section and my responses clearly organized. If I gave one-word or short responses, flesh them out into more complete sentences. Make the writing clear to someone who is reading it and didn't know the questions that were asked. Include the full URLs for all inspiration sites.
  • Copy the final version into the Background section of your Project Brief.
  • Edit the text freely once it is in your document.
  • If you cannot copy the response, use the copy‑friendly request line.
After you finish this background prompt, add the next prompt in the same chat window. That helps make sure That the AI has the correct context to work from.

Content Prompt [prompt 2]

Copy and paste the following into the same AI chat window after completing the background prompt. This version includes enough details about the project requirements so the AI can guide you properly.

I'm planning a multi-page, responsive website for my final Web Design project. Please help me quickly generate a clear *starter content plan* based on the background info I already provided in this chat (audience, mood, style, colors, typography, and inspiration).

My project requirements:

* Topic must be a **Travel/Local Guide**, **Hobby Site**, or **How‑To Guide**
* Minimal original writing; curated/adapted content is fine
* 3–5 pages with consistent navigation
* At least one reused component (ex: cards, repeated sections)
* At least one simple interactive element (ex: accordion, lightbox)
* Images and embedded video encouraged
* Focus is on **design**, clarity, and responsiveness—not heavy content creation

Please guide me through these **short steps**, then create a compact content summary. **At every step, update your plan to match my chosen pages exactly. Do not keep any pages or sections I do not confirm. Always replace earlier suggestions with my final selections.**

### 1. Page List (Fast)

Ask me:

* Which 3–5 pages I want
* One sentence about what each page should do

Then: **Rewrite the page list using ONLY the pages I confirm. Delete all earlier suggestions.**

### 2. Sections (Fast)

For each page in **my confirmed list only**, suggest 2–4 sensible sections based on my topic. Ask me to confirm or adjust.
Examples: hero, intro, highlights, tips, gallery, steps, resources.

After I confirm: **Use only my approved sections and remove any unconfirmed ones.**

### 3. Light Content

For each approved section:

* Provide **very short placeholder text** I can refine later
* Suggest **1–2 possible images or videos** and where to source them (Pexels/YouTube)

### 4. Interaction

Suggest **one simple interactive element** that fits my topic. Ask me where it should go. Use only the location I approve.

### 5. Final Output

Create a short, clear summary with:

* My *final confirmed* page list
* Sections for each page (approved only)
* Short placeholder text
* Suggested media ideas
* Optional simple interaction

**Do not include any pages, sections, or ideas I did not confirm. Replace all earlier suggestions with my final choices.**

Make this concise and beginner‑friendly so I can move quickly into wireframing. Keep the writing brief so I can move quickly into wireframing.
  • Copy the final version into the Content section of your Project Brief.
  • Edit the text freely once it is in your document.
  • If you cannot copy the response, use the copy‑friendly request line.

Prompt for Generating a Simple Site Map Diagram [prompt 3]

After you complete your background prompt and your content prompt, ask Copilot (GPT‑5) to generate a clean, site map image for your project. Copilot can output normal PNG images, so this prompt asks it to draw a simple diagram that looks like a real site map.

Copy and paste the following prompt into the same Copilot chat window where you completed the earlier prompts:

Using the final page list I confirmed earlier, create a **simple black‑and‑white, hierarchical, **site map diagram as a PNG image**. Follow these requirements:

* Put the **Home** page at the top.
* Beneath it, draw **one box for each page** in my final confirmed list.
* If any pages are sub‑pages/child pages, place them under their parent and connect them visually.
* Use clean rectangular boxes and connecting lines.
* Make sure the text labels inside each box are readable and match the pages we agreed on earlier.
* Keep the layout simple and beginner‑friendly.
* **Use ONLY the pages I confirmed**, and remove all earlier suggestions.

After generating the PNG diagram, include a brief one‑sentence explanation of the structure.

Troubleshooting Tips for Copilot (GPT‑5)

If Copilot isn't giving you the correct site map, try these fixes:

  • It added pages I didn't ask for:
    Only use the pages I confirmed. Remove all others and regenerate the diagram.
  • It ignored my sub‑pages:
    Show these as child pages under their parent and connect them visually.
  • It changed my page names:
    Use my exact page names. Do not rename or paraphrase.
  • The diagram is messy or hard to read:
    Please regenerate the PNG using a cleaner layout with evenly spaced boxes.
  • It mixed old and new versions of my pages:
    Forget all previous versions and use ONLY my final confirmed page list from earlier in this chat.

Use these prompts anytime Copilot drifts from your actual project structure. The goal is a simple, accurate PNG site map that matches your pages exactly.

  • Download or copy the image and put it into the Site Map section of your Creative Brief.
  • Edit the text freely once it is in your document.
  • If you cannot copy the response, use the copy‑friendly request line.

Prompt for Choosing a Color Palette [prompt 4]

Copy and paste this prompt into the same AI chat window once your site structure and content plan are done.

Act as my design brainstorming partner. I am creating a website and need help developing a color palette. Please propose 3–4 different palettes. Each palette should include:

* **Primary text color**
* **Primary background color**
* **Link/Button color**
* **Two accent colors** for highlights or features
* A link to **Realtime Colors link** using the HEX values of the colors in the folllowing  format replacing the each color with the actual hex color (no# included): `https://www.realtimecolors.com/?colors=textColorHEX-backgroundColorHEX-linkColorHEX-accentColor1HEX`-accentColor2HEX

For each color, include a short descriptive name and a hex value.

After you give me the palettes, ask me which one I prefer **or** allow me to adjust or replace any color—including bringing my own colors. When I make a change, acknowledge my change and then regenerate the full set of five colors in this order:

1. Text
2. Background
3. Link
4. Secondary
5. Accent

After we finalize the palette, create a **Realtime Colors link** using the format:
https://www.realtimecolors.com/?colors=HEX1-HEX2-HEX3-HEX4-HEX5&fonts=HEADINGFONT-BODYFONT

Make sure hex values appear without #, fonts are separated by dashes, and any spaces become `%20`. Do not choose fonts yet—leave the fonts placeholder intact.

There is no need to copy this output as we will have output with both colors and text after the next propmt.

Typography Pairing Brainstorming Prompt [prompt 5]

Copy and paste this prompt into the same AI chat window once your colors are done. 

Act as my design brainstorming partner. I need help choosing typography for my website. Please propose 3–4 Google Font pairings. For each pairing, include:

* **Heading font name** with a brief description
* **Body font name** with a brief description
* The reasoning behind why the pairing works
* a clickable link to **Realtime Colors link** that uses the colors from our earlier prompt and the font pairing. Every pairing should have the same colors in the same order. Format the link as follows remembering to add %20 in place of any spaces in font names: `https://www.realtimecolors.com/?colors=TEXT-BACKGROUND-LINK-SECONDARY-ACCENT&fonts=HEADINGFONT-BODYFONT`

Use these sources to inform your suggestions:

* https://www.typewolf.com/recommendations
* https://www.figma.com/google-fonts/
* https://www.pagecloud.com/blog/best-google-font-pairings
* https://heyreliable.com/ultimate-google-font-pairings/

After you present the pairings, ask me which one I prefer **or** allow me to bring my own fonts. When I choose or modify fonts, acknowledge my choices and regenerate the final pairing.

Finally, generate a clickable **Realtime Colors link** that uses:

* My finalized color palette (from our color prompt)
* My finalized typography choices

Format the link as:
`https://www.realtimecolors.com/?colors=TEXT-BACKGROUND-LINK-SECONDARY-ACCENT&fonts=HEADINGFONT-BODYFONT`

Replace all spaces in font names with `%20`.

Paste the Output into the Color and Typography section of the document

  • Copy the final version with colors, fonts, pairings, Realtime colors links, and typography notesinto the Color and Typography section of your Creative Brief.
  • Feel free to override the suggestions or bring your own colors and font pairings.
  • If the response is not copyable, paste the copy‑friendly request line.

Project Title and Overview [prompt 6]

Now that you have all of the information, gen AI should be able to help give you some ideas on how to write a summary of what your site is about.

Use this prompt at the end of your planning process to generate the title and short description that will appear at the top of your Creative Brief.

Help me create the top section of my Project 4 Planning Document. First, ask me for a working title for my website — remind me that I can change it later. Then, using everything we’ve developed so far, write a clear 2–3 sentence overview of what my site is about. Keep the tone simple and student-friendly. Once it’s done, present the final output in a clean, copy‑friendly rich‑text format.
  • Write the title at the top of the document next to Project Title
  • Once the 2-3 sentence description is where you like it, copy that and paste it right after the title.
  • Feel free to override the suggestions and edit the text how you like.
  • If the response is not copyable, paste the copy‑friendly request line.
